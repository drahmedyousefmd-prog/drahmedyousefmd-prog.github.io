الجهاز الهضمي

Oral Problems

Gingivitis, herpes, thrush, oral sores

تحذير
Ulcer lasting over 2 weeks, recurrent, or with systemic symptoms — think autoimmune cause or refer.
  • - Vesicles around lip: Herpes simplex
  • - White patches: Mouth thrush
  • - Painful ulcer, white membrane: Oral sore
  • - Red swollen gums: Gingivitis
  • - Culture: Swab of lesions/vesicles for HSV if herpes suspected; oral swab for candida
  • - Special test: Full dental examination; biopsy if ulcer persists > 2 weeks
  • - Monitor: Lesion healing, response to treatment, recurrence (herpes)
  • - Screen: Blood count if recurrent aphthous ulcers (iron, B12, folate deficiency)

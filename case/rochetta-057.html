الجهاز التنفسي

Acute Bronchitis

Cough productive of sputum, often post-viral, ± wheeze

  • - Persistent cough > 5 days, often with mucopurulent sputum
  • - Chest tightness, mild dyspnea on exertion
  • - Chest X-ray normal — to rule out pneumonia
  • - Imaging: Chest X-ray (PA) — exclude pneumonia/consolidation
  • - Lab: CRP, FBC if fever persists or bacterial cause suspected
  • - Culture: Sputum MC&S if purulent and bacterial suspected
  • - Monitor: Cough duration (> 3 weeks needs further review), fever curve, response to mucolytics
  • - Mucolytic to loosen secretions
  • - Cough suppressant only if severely distressing
  • - Antibiotics only if bacterial cause suspected

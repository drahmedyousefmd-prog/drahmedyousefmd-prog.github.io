الرئيسية

Upper RTI / Pharyngitis

Sore throat, fever, cough — mostly viral, selective antibiotics

الرئيسية/Antibiotics/Upper RTI / Pharyngitis
التشخيص
  • - Centor score: fever, tonsillar exudates, tender ALN, no cough
  • - Score 0–1: viral → symptomatic only
  • - Score 2–3: consider rapid strep test
  • - Score 4: empirical Penicillin / Amoxicillin
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Culture: Rapid strep test or throat culture if Centor ≥ 2
  • - Lab: CBC, CRP — distinguish viral vs bacterial if uncertain
  • - Special test: Centor score calculation; no routine imaging for simple URTI
  • - Monitor: Symptom resolution at 48–72h; reassess if worsening
العلاج
  • - Most URTIs are viral — NO antibiotics
  • - Use narrowest spectrum possible
  • - Prescribe for shortest effective duration
  • - Never use fluoroquinolones for simple infections
  • - Penicillin allergy → macrolide or doxycycline
  • - Duration: 5–7 days (not 10–14)

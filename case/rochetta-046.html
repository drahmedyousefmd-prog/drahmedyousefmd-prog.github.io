Thyroid Disorders

Hypo- and hyperthyroidism — metabolic regulation

الرئيسية/Cardiovascular/Thyroid Disorders
التشخيص
  • - Hypothyroidism: Fatigue, weight gain, cold intolerance, TSH elevated
  • - Hyperthyroidism: Weight loss, heat intolerance, tremor, TSH suppressed
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: TSH, free T4, free T3; anti-TPO, anti-TG antibodies (autoimmune)
  • - Imaging: Thyroid ultrasound with scintigraphy (uptake) for thyroiditis vs nodule
  • - ECG: Baseline — tachycardia, atrial fibrillation (hyperthyroid)
  • - Monitor: TSH every 6–8 weeks to dose titrate; FBC on carbimazole (agranulocytosis risk)
العلاج
  • - Hypothyroidism: Levothyroxine — lifelong
  • - Hyperthyroidism: Carbimazole — 12–18 months
⚠ تحذير

Levothyroxine: Take on empty stomach, 30–60 min before breakfast. Avoid with calcium/iron supplements (space 4 hours).

تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Levothyroxine: Take on EMPTY stomach, 30-60 min before breakfast
  • ️ Take with plain water only — not coffee, milk, or juice
  • ️ Space 4 hours from calcium, iron, PPIs, or antacids
  • ️ Consistency is key — same time daily
  • ️ Carbimazole: Same time daily
  • ️ Regular TSH monitoring every 6-8 weeks until stable
  • ️ Report palpitations, weight loss (overtreatment) or fatigue, weight gain (undertreatment)
  • Do NOT stop Carbimazole suddenly
  • ️ Report sore throat, fever, mouth ulcers on Carbimazole (blood disorder — rare but serious)

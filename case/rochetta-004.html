Schizophrenia

Hallucinations, delusions, disorganized thinking — chronic psychotic illness

تحذير
Metabolic monitoring: 2nd-gen antipsychotics cause weight gain, diabetes, dyslipidemia. Monitor weight, FBS, lipids quarterly. Olanzapine: highest metabolic risk.
  • - Positive: Hallucinations (auditory), delusions, thought disorder
  • - Negative: Flat affect, social withdrawal, amotivation
  • - Duration ≥ 6 months with active symptoms ≥ 1 month
  • - First episode: rule out organic causes (drugs, metabolic)
  • - Lab: CBC, FBS, LFTs, renal function, TSH, B12, urine drug screen
  • - Imaging: CT/MRI brain if first episode or atypical presentation — exclude structural cause
  • - Monitor: Weight, FBS, lipids, prolactin quarterly (2nd-gen antipsychotics)
  • - ECG: Baseline and repeat if QT-prolonging drugs used
  • - 1st gen: Haloperidol — positive symptoms mainly
  • - 2nd gen: Risperidone / Olanzapine / Quetiapine
  • - Long-acting: Paliperidone depot — adherence issues
  • - Clozapine: Refractory schizophrenia — last resort

التفاعلات الدوائية
Increased risk of Torsades de Pointes. ECG monitoring recommended.
Risk of severe hypotension and respiratory depression. Avoid.
Increased Risperidone levels. Dose adjustment may be needed.
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Do NOT stop antipsychotics suddenly — risk of relapse
  • ️ Metabolic monitoring: weight, blood sugar, lipids every 3-6 months
  • ️ Olanzapine carries highest metabolic risk
  • Take Risperidone at the same time daily
  • Take Olanzapine/Quetiapine at bedtime (sedating)
  • ️ Report any unusual movements (tardive dyskinesia) immediately
  • ️ Report fever + muscle rigidity immediately (NMS — emergency)
  • Avoid alcohol — worsens symptoms
  • Avoid overheating and dehydration (especially Clozapine)

Sinusitis

Acute or chronic inflammation of paranasal sinuses

الرئيسية/Respiratory/Sinusitis
التشخيص
  • - Facial pain/pressure over affected sinus
  • - Nasal congestion, purulent rhinorrhea, anosmia
  • - Tenderness on palpation of maxillary/ethmoid sinuses
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Imaging: CT sinuses if recurrent or refractory; plain X-ray (OP view) for air-fluid levels
  • - Lab: CRP, WCC if bacterial severity questioned
  • - Culture: Endoscopically-guided swab if purulent discharge; allergy testing if chronic
  • - Monitor: Symptom resolution at 7–10 days, treatment response, recurrence rate
العلاج
  • - Nasal saline irrigation — clears secretions
  • - Nasal decongestant — max 3–5 days (rebound risk)
  • - Antibiotics if symptoms > 10 days or worsening

Insomnia

Difficulty initiating or maintaining sleep — poor sleep hygiene most common cause

  • - Most insomnia is secondary: stress, depression, medications
  • - Sleep hygiene FIRST: regular schedule, no screens, no caffeine PM
  • - CBT-I is most effective long-term treatment
  • - Pharmacotherapy: short-term only (2–4 weeks)
  • - Lab: TSH, CBC, FBS, B12, ferritin — exclude organic sleep disorders
  • - Special test: Sleep diary (2 weeks), Epworth Sleepiness Scale; PSG if suspected OSA
  • - Monitor: Benzodiazepine duration, rebound insomnia, daytime sedation
  • - Screen: Alcohol use, depression screen (PHQ-9), medication review for sleep disruptors
  • - Zolpidem 5-10mg: Short-acting — sleep onset insomnia
  • - Zopiclone 3.75-7.5mg: Alternative to zolpidem
  • - Melatonin 2mg: Jet lag, shift work, elderly
  • - Amitriptyline 10-25mg: If comorbid depression/pain

Atrial Fibrillation

Irregularly irregular pulse — rate vs rhythm control

الرئيسية/Cardiovascular/Atrial Fibrillation
التشخيص
  • - Irregularly irregular pulse on examination
  • - ECG: absent P waves, irregular RR intervals
  • - CHADS₂-VASc score for stroke risk → anticoagulation decision
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - ECG: 12-lead to confirm AF (absent P waves, irregular RR); 24h Holter if paroxysmal
  • - Lab: Thyroid function (hyperthyroid cause), renal function, LFTs, electrolytes, FBC
  • - Imaging: Echocardiogram — LA size, valvular disease, LV function
  • - Monitor: INR (warfarin) or renal function (DOACs), heart rate, bleeding signs
العلاج
  • - Rate control: Beta-blocker or CCB
  • - Anticoagulation: DOAC preferred (Rivaroxaban/Apixaban)
  • - Valvular AF: Warfarin only
⚠ تحذير

Rate control target: Resting HR < 110 bpm. Beta-blocker or CCB — avoid Verapamil with Beta-blocker.

⚠ التفاعلات الدوائية

تحذيرات إضافية
  • Warfarin + NSAIDs:
  • ↑↑ Bleeding risk. Use Paracetamol instead.
  • Warfarin + Amiodarone:
  • ↑↑ Significantly increased INR. Reduce Warfarin 30-50% and monitor INR.
  • Warfarin + Antibiotics (Metronidazole, Ciprofloxacin):
  • ️↑ INR increases. Monitor INR every 3-5 days during course.
  • DOACs + Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (Ketoconazole, Ritonavir):
  • ️↑↑ Drug levels and bleeding risk. Avoid.
  • DOACs + Strong CYP3A4 inducers (Rifampin, Carbamazepine):
  • ↓↓ Reduced efficacy → thrombosis. Avoid.
  • Beta-blockers + Verapamil/Diltiazem:
  • Severe bradycardia and heart block.
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Warfarin: Same time daily. Regular INR monitoring (target 2-3)
  • ️ Rivaroxaban: Take WITH food
  • ️ Apixaban: Can be taken with or without food
  • ️ Report ANY signs of bleeding: unusual bruising, blood in urine/stool
  • ️ Maintain CONSISTENT diet — changes in Vitamin K affect Warfarin
  • Take at the same time daily
  • ️ If you miss DOAC dose: take if <12h late (Apixaban) or <6h (Rivaroxaban)
  • Do NOT stop anticoagulation without specialist advice — stroke risk
  • ️ Wear medical alert bracelet on Warfarin
  • ️ Inform ALL healthcare providers about anticoagulation before procedures

Bipolar Affective Disorder

Alternating manic and depressive episodes — lithium remains gold standard

الرئيسية/Psychiatry/Bipolar Affective Disorder
التشخيص
  • - Mania: elevated mood, pressured speech, grandiosity, decreased need for sleep
  • - Depression: as per MDD criteria
  • - Most patients present during depressive episode
  • - CRITICAL: screen for bipolar before starting antidepressant
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: TSH, FBS, CBC, LFTs, renal function, lithium baseline levels
  • - Special test: YMRS (mania), PHQ-9 (depression), drug screen to exclude substance-induced mood
  • - Monitor: Lithium levels every 3–6 months, renal function, thyroid function, weight
  • - ECG: Baseline if on antipsychotics or with cardiac risk
العلاج
  • - 1st line: Lithium — prophylaxis + acute mania
  • - Alternative: Valproic acid — rapid cycling
  • - Quetiapine / Olanzapine — acute mania + mixed
  • - Lamotrigine — bipolar depression maintenance
  • - NEVER use antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar
⚠ تحذير

Lithium monitoring: Therapeutic index narrow (0.6–0.8 mmol/L). Check renal function, thyroid, and lithium levels regularly. Toxicity: tremor, vomiting, confusion → medical emergency.

Acute Bacterial Sinusitis

Facial pain, purulent nasal discharge, nasal obstruction > 10 days

الرئيسية/Antibiotics/Acute Bacterial Sinusitis
التشخيص
  • - Persistent symptoms > 10 days without improvement
  • - "Double worsening" — initial improvement then deterioration
  • - Severe symptoms > 3–4 days with high fever
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Imaging: CT sinuses (water–bone window) if recurrent or refractory; plain X-ray (OP view) shows air-fluid levels
  • - Lab: CBC, CRP — assess severity of bacterial infection
  • - Culture: Nasal endoscopy with swab if purulent drainage; allergy testing if chronic
  • - Monitor: Symptom resolution at 7–10 days; treatment response
العلاج
  • - Mild: Nasal saline + decongestant × 5–7 days
  • - Moderate: Amoxicillin 1g TDS × 5–7 days
  • - Severe / Refractory: Augmentin 1g BID × 7 days
  • - Add intranasal corticosteroid (Fluticasone)

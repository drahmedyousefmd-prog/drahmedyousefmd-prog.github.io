Acute Gout

Sudden onset monoarthritis — especially 1st MTP — crystal arthropathy

الرئيسية/Pain & Analgesics/Acute Gout
التشخيص
  • - Excruciating pain at base of great toe (podagra)
  • - Hot, swollen, tender joint — erythematous overlying skin
  • - Often at night, after dietary excess or dehydration
  • - MSU crystal aspiration = gold standard
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: CBC, ESR/CRP, serum uric acid (may be normal during attack), renal function, LFTs
  • - Special test: MSU crystal aspiration under polarised microscopy (gold standard)
  • - Imaging: X-ray of affected joint to exclude fractures or calcification
  • - Monitor: Serum urate levels during prophylaxis (target < 360 µmol/L)
العلاج
  • - ACUTE ATTACK: NSAID (high dose) OR Colchicine (low dose) OR Steroid
  • - PROPHYLAXIS (if ≥ 2 attacks/year): Allopurinol 100mg → titrate to target urate < 360
  • - Lifestyle: reduce red meat, organ meats, beer; increase water
  • - Start Allopurinol 2-4 weeks AFTER attack settles

تحذيرات إضافية

  • ↑↑↑ Life-threatening bone marrow suppression. Reduce Azathioprine by 75% or avoid.

  • ↑↑↑ Fatal toxicity (especially with renal impairment). Avoid completely.
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Allopurinol: Start 2-4 weeks AFTER attack settles
  • ️ Colchicine: Take at FIRST sign of attack — most effective within 12h
  • ️ Colchicine max: 6mg total per attack
  • ️ Maintain high fluid intake (2-3L/day)
  • Take with food
  • Avoid alcohol — increases uric acid
  • Avoid organ meats, red meat, beer
  • ️ Report rash, mouth sores, or fever (serious side effects)

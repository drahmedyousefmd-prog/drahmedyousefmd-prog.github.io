Cellulitis & SSTI

Spreading skin infection — erythema, warmth, tenderness, lymphangitis

الرئيسية/Antibiotics/Cellulitis & SSTI
التشخيص
  • - Spreading erythema with well-defined border
  • - Warmth, tenderness, edema
  • - Lymphangitis (red streaking) — lymphadenopathy
  • - Look for portal of entry: cracks, eczema, ulcers
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: CBC, CRP/ESR, blood cultures if systemic toxicity
  • - Culture: Wound swab for culture and sensitivity if pus or non-resolving
  • - Monitor: Temperature, erythema borders (mark with pen), WCC, renal function
  • - Imaging: Ultrasound if abscess suspected (fluctuance, pointing)
العلاج
  • - Mild (oral): Flucloxacillin or Cephalexin × 7 days
  • - Moderate: IV Flucloxacillin or Ceftriaxone
  • - Penicillin allergy: Clindamycin or Doxycycline

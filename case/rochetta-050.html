Renal Colic / Urolithiasis

Severe colicky flank pain radiating to groin — kidney stones

الرئيسية/Urinary/Renal Colic / Urolithiasis
التشخيص
  • - Severe colicky pain — comes in waves, intermits
  • - Radiates from flank to groin (loin to groin)
  • - Nausea, vomiting, hematuria
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Imaging: CT KUB (non-contrast) — stone size, location, obstruction
  • - Lab: CBC, CRP, renal function, electrolytes, serum urate/calcium; urine dipstick (haematuria)
  • - Culture: Urine MC&S if infection suspected (obstructing infected stone = emergency)
  • - Monitor: Pain score, urine output, stone passage; renal function if obstruction
العلاج
  • - IV fluids if vomiting / dehydrated
  • - Strong analgesic — Ketorolac IM or Nalbuphine
  • - Antispasmodic — Drotaverine or Hyoscine
  • - Stone passage: Tamsulosin (medical expulsive therapy)
⚠ تحذير

Infected obstructing stone is a urological emergency — decompress with stent or percutaneous nephrostomy before antibiotics can work.

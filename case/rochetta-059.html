الجهاز التنفسي

COPD

Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease — smoker, progressive dyspnea

تحذير
Controlled oxygen therapy — high-flow O2 in COPD can cause CO2 retention and respiratory failure. Target SpO2 88–92%.
  • - Smoker or ex-smoker, age > 40
  • - Progressive dyspnea, chronic cough, sputum production
  • - Post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC < 0.70 confirms
  • - Spirometry: Pre-and-post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC < 0.70 (irreversible obstruction)
  • - Imaging: Chest X-ray — hyperinflation, bullae; exclude other pathology
  • - Lab: Alpha-1 antitrypsin (early-onset/emphysema), FBS if on steroids
  • - Monitor: FEV1 trend annually, SpO2 (target 88–92%), exacerbation frequency, symptoms
  • - LAMA first — Tiotropium (Spiriva) for all GOLD stages
  • - Add LABA if symptoms persist
  • - Exacerbation: Prednisolone 40mg × 5d + Bronchodilators

التفاعلات الدوائية
↑↑ GI ulcer/bleeding risk. Add PPI.
May worsen bronchospasm.
Additive anticholinergic effects (dry mouth, urinary retention).
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Prednisolone 5-day course: Complete then STOP — do not taper <5 days
  • ️ Tiotropium: Load capsule, pierce once, inhale deeply
  • ️ RINSE mouth after Budesonide — prevents oral thrush
  • ️ Ventolin: Shake well before each puff
  • ️ Controlled oxygen: Target SpO2 88-92% — too much O2 dangerous in COPD
  • Take Tiotropium at same time each day
  • ️ Get annual flu and pneumococcal vaccines
  • Do NOT smoke — most important intervention
  • ️ If rescue inhaler needed >2 times/week, see doctor

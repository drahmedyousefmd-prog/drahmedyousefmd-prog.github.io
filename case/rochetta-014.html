Migraine

Unilateral throbbing headache ± aura, photophobia, nausea

الرئيسية/Pain & Analgesics/Migraine
التشخيص
  • - Unilateral, pulsating headache 4–72 hours
  • - With or without aura (visual disturbances)
  • - Photophobia, phonophobia, nausea/vomiting
  • - Triggers: stress, hormones, sleep deprivation, alcohol
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: CBC, TSH, FBS — rule out secondary causes if atypical pattern
  • - Special test: Headache diary; consider CT/MRI brain if first or worst headache, focal neurological signs
  • - ECG: Baseline if starting propranolol prophylaxis
  • - Monitor: Migraine frequency, severity (VAS), medication overuse (analgesic headache)
العلاج
  • - Mild-Moderate: Paracetamol + NSAID + antiemetic
  • - Moderate-Severe: Triptan (Sumatriptan 50mg) + NSAID
  • - Prophylaxis: Propranolol / Amitriptyline / Topiramate

⚠ التفاعلات الدوائية

⚠️ Serotonin Syndrome risk. Monitor but may be used with caution.

❌ Severe bradycardia, heart block, heart failure.

⚠️ Masks hypoglycemia symptoms. Monitor glucose closely.

تحذيرات إضافية
  • Triptans + SSRIs/MAOIs:
  • ️ Serotonin Syndrome risk. Monitor but may be used with caution.
  • Propranolol + Verapamil/Diltiazem:
  • Severe bradycardia, heart block, heart failure.
  • Propranolol + Insulin/Sulfonylureas:
  • ️ Masks hypoglycemia symptoms. Monitor glucose closely.
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Sumatriptan: Take at EARLIEST sign of migraine — most effective within 1h
  • ️ Max 2 doses in 24 hours
  • ️ Propranolol (prophylaxis): Takes 2-4 weeks for full effect
  • ️ Propranolol: Do NOT stop suddenly — taper over 1-2 weeks
  • Take Paracetamol + Ibuprofen at onset for best results
  • Avoid alcohol, chocolate, aged cheese — triggers
  • ️ If asthmatic: use Amlodipine instead of Propranolol

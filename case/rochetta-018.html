Community-Acquired Pneumonia

Productive cough, fever, pleuritic chest pain, consolidation on CXR

الرئيسية/Antibiotics/Community-Acquired Pneumonia
التشخيص
  • - CURB-65: Confusion, Urea > 7, RR ≥ 30, BP < 90/60, Age ≥ 65
  • - Score 0–1: outpatient; 2: consider admission; 3–5: ICU
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Imaging: Chest X-ray PA (lobar consolidation, air bronchograms)
  • - Lab: CBC, CRP/ESR, blood cultures if febrile, sputum MC&S, renal function
  • - Special test: CURB-65 score calculation; pulse oximetry (SpO2)
  • - Culture: Blood cultures before antibiotics if admitted
العلاج
  • - Mild (CURB 0–1): Oral Amoxicillin 1g TDS × 5 days
  • - Moderate (CURB 2): Amoxicillin + Azithromycin
  • - Severe (CURB ≥3): IV Co-amoxiclav + Azithromycin
  • - ICU care — consider Vanc + Ceftriaxone if MRSA risk

⚠ التفاعلات الدوائية

❌ ↑↑ Rhabdomyolysis risk. Stop statin during Clarithromycin course.

⚠️ Increased INR. Monitor every 3-5 days.

تحذيرات إضافية
  • Clarithromycin + Statins (Simvastatin, Atorvastatin):
  • ↑↑ Rhabdomyolysis risk. Stop statin during Clarithromycin course.
  • Clarithromycin + Warfarin:
  • ️ Increased INR. Monitor every 3-5 days.
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Complete FULL antibiotic course — critical for pneumonia
  • ️ Augmentin: Take with food to reduce GI upset
  • ️ Azithromycin: Take 1h before or 2h after food
  • Take Paracetamol regularly for fever
  • Drink plenty of fluids — helps thin secretions
  • ️ If not improving in 48-72h, return to doctor
  • ️ Fluimucil: Dissolve in water, drink immediately
  • Do NOT use leftover antibiotics from previous illness

Edema / Diuretic Therapy

Peripheral edema, heart failure, liver disease, nephrotic syndrome

الرئيسية/Urinary/Edema / Diuretic Therapy
التشخيص
  • - Cardiac: Heart failure — pitting, bilateral, ankles
  • - Hepatic: Liver cirrhosis — ascites + pedal edema
  • - Renal: Nephrotic syndrome — periorbital + generalized
  • - Drugs: CCBs, NSAIDs, steroids
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: Renal function, electrolytes, LFTs, albumin, urinalysis (proteinuria) — delineate cause
  • - Imaging: Echocardiogram (cardiac), liver ultrasound (cirrhosis/ascites)
  • - Special test: 24h urine protein if nephrotic syndrome; JVP assessment for cardiac cause
  • - Monitor: Daily weight, fluid balance, electrolytes (Na+/K+), renal function on diuretics
العلاج
  • - Mild edema: Thiazides (HCTZ) or K-sparing (Spironolactone)
  • - Moderate: Loop diuretics (Furosemide 20–40mg)
  • - Severe: High-dose loop ± thiazide combination
⚠ تحذير

Spironolactone + ACE inhibitors → hyperkalemia risk. Furosemide + Digoxin → hypokalemia → toxicity.

تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Furosemide: Take in the MORNING — prevents nighttime urination
  • ️ Spironolactone: Take at the same time daily
  • ️ Monitor daily weight — stable weight = good fluid balance
  • ️ Report weight gain >1kg/day or >2kg/week
  • ️ Monitor for muscle cramps, weakness (electrolyte imbalance)
  • Furosemide: Best on empty stomach
  • ️ Regular blood tests: electrolytes, kidney function
  • Do NOT take potassium supplements unless prescribed
  • Avoid NSAIDs — reduce diuretic effect
  • ️ Report irregular heartbeat, muscle weakness

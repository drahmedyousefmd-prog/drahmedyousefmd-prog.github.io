Fungal Skin Infections

Tinea corporis, tinea pedis, candidiasis — dermatophytes & yeasts

الرئيسية/Dermatology/Fungal Skin Infections
التشخيص
  • - Tinea corporis: Annular scaly plaque, central clearing
  • - Tinea pedis: Interdigital maceration, scaling
  • - Candidiasis: Satellite pustules, white curd
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Culture: KOH preparation of skin scraping (dermatophyte identification)
  • - Special test: Fungal culture if KOH negative but suspicion high; Wood's lamp for tinea capitis
  • - Lab: FBS if recurrent tinea (diabetes risk), HIV screen if widespread/refractory
  • - Monitor: Treatment response at 2 weeks, adherence to topical therapy
العلاج
  • - Local (mild): Terbinafine or Clotrimazole cream × 1–2 wks
  • - Widespread: Oral Terbinafine × 2–4 wks
  • - Candidiasis: Nystatin or Clotrimazole cream

Spastic Colon / IBS

Recurrent abdominal pain with altered bowel habit

الرئيسية/GIT/Spastic Colon / IBS
التشخيص
  • - Colicky pain: Mebeverine, Trimebutine
  • - Diarrhea-dominant: Streptoquin, Loperamide with caution
  • - Constipation-dominant: Lactulose, bulk-forming agents
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: CBC, TSH, FBS, coeliac serology (tTG-IgA), ESR/CRP — exclude organic causes
  • - Culture: Stool MC&S if infectious diarrhea suspected
  • - Special test: IBS diagnostic criteria (Rome IV); colonoscopy if alarm features or age > 50
  • - Monitor: Stool diary (Bristol scale), symptom frequency, response to antispasmodic
العلاج
  • - Small frequent meals, avoid large ones
  • - Reduce caffeine and stimulants
  • - Regular physical activity, good sleep
⚠ تحذير

Rifaximin is not a general IBS treatment — proven role only in IBS-D with suspected bacterial overgrowth.

Dyslipidemia

Elevated LDL / Triglycerides — cardiovascular risk reduction

الرئيسية/Cardiovascular/Dyslipidemia
التشخيص
  • - Low CV risk: LDL < 3.0 mmol/L
  • - Moderate risk: LDL < 2.0 mmol/L
  • - High risk (DM, PAD): LDL < 1.8 mmol/L
  • - Very high (AMI, stroke): LDL < 1.4 mmol/L
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: Fasting lipid profile (LDL, HDL, triglycerides), LFTs baseline before statin, renal function
  • - Special test: CV risk calculation (SCORE2/QRISK); HbA1c/FBS to exclude DM
  • - Monitor: LFTs 3 months after starting statin, then annually; lipid profile on target
  • - Screen: Rule out secondary dyslipidemia (hypothyroidism, diabetes, nephrotic syndrome)
العلاج
  • - Atorvastatin: Most potent — 20–80mg
  • - Ezetimibe: Add-on to statin for extra LDL reduction
  • - Fenofibrate: First choice for triglycerides > 500
  • - Check LFTs before starting

⚠ التفاعلات الدوائية

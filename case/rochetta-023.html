Hospital-Acquired Infection

HAP/VAP, UTI, Bacteremia — broad-spectrum empirical therapy

الرئيسية/Antibiotics/Hospital-Acquired Infection
التشخيص
  • - Most common: MRSA, Pseudomonas, ESBL organisms
  • - Check local antibiogram before prescribing
  • - Obtain cultures BEFORE starting antibiotics
  • - De-escalate once sensitivities available
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Culture: Blood cultures ×2 (before antibiotics), sputum/urine/wound cultures as site-appropriate
  • - Lab: CBC, CRP/PCT, renal function, LFTs, electrolytes
  • - Special test: Antibiotic sensitivity results to guide de-escalation; procalcitonin for stewardship
  • - Monitor: Temperature curve, WCC, CRP trend, renal function daily
العلاج
  • - HAP/VAP: Piperacillin-Tazobactam or Meropenem
  • - Gram-positive: Vancomycin — MRSA coverage
  • - Neutropenic sepsis: Meropenem ± Vancomycin

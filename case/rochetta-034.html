Herpes Simplex

Grouped vesicles — orolabial (HSV-1) or genital (HSV-2)

الرئيسية/Dermatology/Herpes Simplex
التشخيص
  • - Grouped vesicles on erythematous base — orolabial (HSV-1) or genital (HSV-2)
  • - Recurrences triggered by stress, sun, fever, menstruation
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Special test: Viral PCR of vesicular fluid to confirm HSV type (HSV-1 vs HSV-2)
  • - Lab: HIV screen if recurrent or severe; type-specific serology for HSV if subclinical
  • - Monitor: Recurrence frequency, response to suppressive therapy, pregnancy status (if applicable)
  • - Biopsy: Tzanck smear (rapid, less sensitive) — confirmatory PCR preferred
العلاج
  • - Episodic therapy at prodrome (tingling) — best response
  • - Suppressive therapy if > 6 recurrences/year
⚠ تحذير

Genital herpes — counsel on transmission and partner notification. In pregnancy — specialist input required.

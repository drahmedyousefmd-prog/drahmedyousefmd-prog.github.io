Scabies

Intense nocturnal pruritus — Sarcoptes scabiei mite

  • - Intense itch worse at night
  • - Burrows in web spaces, wrists, axillae
  • - Secondary excoriation, impetiginization
  • - Close contacts must be treated simultaneously
  • - Special test: Dermoscopy of burrows (triangle pattern); skin scraping for mite identification
  • - Culture: Skin scraping in mineral oil — mite/eggs/ova on microscopy
  • - Monitor: Resolution of itch at 2 weeks, household contact treatment compliance
  • - Screen: Secondary impetiginisation (wound swab if pustular), scabies in contacts
  • - Permethrin 5%: Apply from neck down, leave 8–12 hrs, wash off
  • - Ivermectin: 200mcg/kg PO single dose — alternative
  • - Treat ALL household contacts
  • - Wash bedding/clothing at 60°C

التفاعلات الدوائية
Wait for permethrin to dry before applying other products.
May increase INR. Monitor.
Reduced Ivermectin effectiveness.
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Permethrin: Apply from NECK DOWN — leave 8-12 hours, then wash off
  • ️ REPEAT treatment after 1 WEEK — kills newly hatched mites
  • ️ Treat ALL household contacts simultaneously
  • ️ Wash all bedding/clothing at 60°C or dry clean
  • ️ Eurax: Apply 3 times, leave 24h each time
  • Cetirizine: Take for itching
  • ️ itching may continue for 2-4 weeks after successful treatment (allergic reaction to dead mites)
  • ️ If still itching after 4 weeks, seek reassessment

Renal Colic

Severe loin-to-groin pain — ureteric stone

الرئيسية/Pain & Analgesics/Renal Colic
التشخيص
  • - Severe, colicky flank pain radiating to groin
  • - Nausea, vomiting, restlessness
  • - Haematuria (macro or micro)
  • - Diagnosis: non-contrast CT KUB (gold standard)
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Imaging: Non-contrast CT KUB (gold standard for stone detection and location)
  • - Lab: CBC, CRP, renal function, electrolytes, serum calcium/urate, urinalysis (haematuria)
  • - Culture: Urine MC&S if infection suspected
  • - Monitor: Pain score, urine output, stone size on imaging for passage decision
العلاج
  • - 1st line analgesia: Ketorolac 30mg IM — NSAID superior to opioid
  • - If NSAID fails: Diclofenac IM or Tramadol IV
  • - Antispasmodic: Drotaverine 80mg IM/IV
  • - Medical expulsive: Tamsulosin 0.4mg — for stones 5–10mm

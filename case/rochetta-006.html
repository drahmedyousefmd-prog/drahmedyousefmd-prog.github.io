ADHD

Inattention, hyperactivity, impulsivity — children and adults

الرئيسية/Psychiatry/ADHD
التشخيص
  • - Inattention: difficulty sustaining tasks, forgetfulness
  • - Hyperactivity: fidgeting, restlessness
  • - Impulsivity: interrupting, difficulty waiting turn
  • - Onset before age 12, present in ≥ 2 settings
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: CBC, FBS, TSH, LFTs — rule out thyroid/metabolic mimic
  • - Special test: Conners' rating scale (children), ASRS (adults); echocardiogram if cardiac history
  • - Monitor: Height, weight, appetite, heart rate, BP before titrating stimulant doses
  • - ECG: Baseline if family history of cardiac disease or palpitations
العلاج
  • - Children: Methylphenidate 1st line
  • - Adults: Lisdexamfetamine or Methylphenidate
  • - Non-stimulant: Atomoxetine (SNRI)
  • - Psychoeducation: Essential for all patients + behavioral therapy

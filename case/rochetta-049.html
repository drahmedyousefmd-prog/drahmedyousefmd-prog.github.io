Upper UTI / Pyelonephritis

Fever, flank pain, nausea — infection ascending to kidney

الرئيسية/Urinary/Upper UTI / Pyelonephritis
التشخيص
  • - Fever > 38.5°C, rigors, flank pain (CVA tenderness)
  • - Nausea, vomiting, malaise
  • - Blood cultures if sepsis suspected
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Culture: Urine MC&S + blood cultures (if febrile/septic) before antibiotics
  • - Lab: CBC, CRP, renal function, electrolytes — assess severity and guide therapy
  • - Imaging: Renal ultrasound — exclude obstruction, renal abscess, calculi
  • - Monitor: Temperature, CVA tenderness, response to antibiotics at 48h; repeat if refractory
العلاج
  • - Mild-Moderate: Oral antibiotics 10–14 days
  • - Severe / Sepsis: IV antibiotics + admit
  • - Oral (mild): Ciprofloxacin 500mg BID × 7-14 days
  • - IV (severe): Ceftriaxone 1g OD IV
⚠ تحذير

Recurrent pyelonephritis: Investigate for structural abnormality (US renal, CT KUB) — rule out stones, obstruction, reflux.

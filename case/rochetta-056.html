الجهاز التنفسي

Common Cold / URTI

Viral upper respiratory tract infection — self-limiting 7–10 days

تحذير
Do NOT prescribe antibiotics for uncomplicated viral URTI — promotes resistance without benefit.
  • - Nasal congestion, sneezing, rhinorrhea, sore throat
  • - Low-grade fever, malaise, myalgia
  • - No antibiotics needed — viral cause in 95% of cases
  • - Lab: None routinely — clinically diagnosed; CRP if bacterial superinfection uncertain
  • - Special test: Rapid flu/COVID test if influenza epidemics or high-risk patient
  • - Monitor: Temperature, symptom duration (resolve 7–10 days), hydration
  • - Screen: Red flags (high fever > 5 days, pleuritic pain, shortness of breath) for pneumonia
  • - Paracetamol for fever and myalgia
  • - Nasal decongestant for congestion (max 3–5 days)
  • - Antihistamine for sneezing and rhinorrhea
  • - Fluids, rest, humidified air

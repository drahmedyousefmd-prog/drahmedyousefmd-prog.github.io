Liver Support

Adjunct supplements — not a substitute for treating the cause

  • - Silymarin: Regeneration + antioxidant
  • - Glutathione: Toxin protection
  • - Lecithin: Removes fat deposits
  • - Lab: LFTs (ALT/AST), bilirubin, GGT, viral hepatitis screen (HBsAg, anti-HCV)
  • - Imaging: Abdominal ultrasound — steatosis, cirrhosis, portal hypertension
  • - Special test: Fibroscan (liver stiffness) if fibrosis suspected; lipid profile
  • - Monitor: LFTs, symptom response, alcohol intake, weight (NAFLD)
  • - These are supportive only
  • - The underlying cause — virus, fatty liver, toxin — must be diagnosed and treated

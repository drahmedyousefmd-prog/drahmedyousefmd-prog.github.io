Pharyngitis & Tonsillitis

Sore throat — viral vs bacterial (Streptococcus)

الرئيسية/Respiratory/Pharyngitis & Tonsillitis
التشخيص
  • - Centor Criteria: Fever > 38°C (+1), Tonsillar exudates (+1), Tender anterior cervical nodes (+1), Absence of cough (+1)
  • - Score 0–1: Viral — symptomatic only
  • - Score 4: Empiric antibiotics
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Culture: Rapid strep test or throat culture if Centor ≥ 2 (Group A Streptococcus)
  • - Lab: CRP/FBC if systemic features or severity assessment
  • - Special test: Centor score calculation to guide antibiotic decision
  • - Monitor: Symptom resolution at 48–72h, fever, tonsillar exudate; watch for peritonsillar abscess
العلاج
  • - Analgesic / antipyretic — first priority
  • - Antibiotics only if Group A Strep confirmed/suspected
  • - Antibiotics prevent rheumatic fever — the main goal
⚠ تحذير

Penicillin V remains first-line for Strep throat — 10-day course is essential to prevent rheumatic fever.

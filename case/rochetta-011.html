Moderate-Severe Pain

Tramadol, codeine — bridge between mild and strong opioids

  • - NSAIDs/paracetamol alone insufficient
  • - Post-surgical pain (first 48–72h)
  • - Fractures, renal colic, severe dental
  • - Step up from Step 1 → Step 2 WHO ladder
  • - Lab: CBC, LFTs, renal function, urine drug screen if abuse suspected
  • - Special test: Pain assessment scale; review for substance misuse risk before opioids
  • - Monitor: Respiratory rate, sedation level, pupil size, constipation on opioids
  • - ECG: If combining Tramadol with SSRIs (serotonin syndrome risk)
  • - Tramadol: 50-100mg QID — weak mu-agonist + SNRI
  • - Codeine: 30-60mg QID — prodrug (CYP2D6)
  • - Tramadol: seizure risk, serotonin syndrome
  • - Codeine: avoid in children < 12 (respiratory depression)

الجهاز التنفسي

Allergic Rhinitis

Seasonal or perennial — sneezing, itching, watery rhinorrhea

  • - Sneezing, nasal itching, clear watery discharge
  • - Nasal congestion (bilateral)
  • - Positive skin prick test or specific IgE
  • - Special test: Skin prick testing or specific serum IgE (common aeroallergens)
  • - Lab: FBC (eosinophilia), total IgE if systemic allergy suspected
  • - Imaging: Not routinely; consider CT sinuses if coexisting chronic sinusitis
  • - Monitor: Symptom control (sneeze/itch/congestion), adherence to nasal steroid
  • - Mild intermittent: Antihistamine PRN
  • - Moderate-severe: Daily antihistamine + Nasal steroid
  • - Persistent: Add leukotriene modifier (Montelukast)

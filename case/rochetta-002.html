Generalized Anxiety Disorder

Excessive worry ≥ 6 months — muscle tension, restlessness, fatigue

الرئيسية/Psychiatry/Generalized Anxiety Disorder
التشخيص
  • - Persistent, excessive worry about multiple events
  • - Restlessness, being on edge
  • - Muscle tension, difficulty concentrating
  • - Sleep disturbance, irritability
  • - Symptoms present ≥ 6 months
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: CBC, FBS, TSH, LFTs, B12 — exclude thyroid disease, anaemia, metabolic causes
  • - Special test: GAD-7 severity score; rule out substance use (drug screen)
  • - Monitor: Anxiety severity (GAD-7), benzodiazepine use, sleep quality at each visit
  • - ECG: Baseline if initiating TCAs or cardiac comorbidity
العلاج
  • - 1st line: SSRI/SNRI — Sertraline, Escitalopram, Duloxetine
  • - Short-term: Benzodiazepine (max 2–4 weeks)
  • - Psychological: CBT — most effective non-pharm
  • - Adjunct: Pregabalin, Hydroxyzine, Buspirone
⚠ تحذير

Benzodiazepines: Only for short-term (2–4 weeks max). Risk of dependence, tolerance, withdrawal. Never combine with opioids or alcohol. Elderly: increased fall risk, confusion.

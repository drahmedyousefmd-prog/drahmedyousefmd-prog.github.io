Acute Gastroenteritis

Adult patient, less than 14 days of watery diarrhea

الرئيسية/GIT/Acute Gastroenteritis
التشخيص
  • - Acute watery diarrhea, less than 14 days duration
  • - May be associated with nausea, vomiting, colicky abdominal pain
  • - Fever + blood in stool suggests invasive bacterial cause
  • - Check hydration status: skin turgor, capillary refill
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: Electrolytes (Na+/K+ — dehydration), renal function; FBC if bloody diarrhea
  • - Culture: Stool MC&S if bloody/persistent diarrhea or invasive cause suspected
  • - Special test: Hydration assessment (skin turgor, capillary refill, urine output)
  • - Monitor: Fluid intake/output, weight, stool frequency, fever; reassess hydration
العلاج
  • - Rehydration is the real treatment, not antibiotics
  • - Oral rehydration solution after every loose stool
  • - Antispasmodic for colic, antipyretic if fever present
  • - Antibiotics only if invasive bacterial cause suspected
⚠ تحذير

Do not give Loperamide if fever or blood in stool present — risk of toxic megacolon.

⚠ التفاعلات الدوائية

❌ Disulfiram-like reaction: nausea, vomiting, flushing. Avoid alcohol 48h after.

❌ Severely reduced absorption. Space 2-4 hours.

⚠️ Increased cardiac risk.

  • Disulfiram-like reaction: nausea, vomiting, flushing. Avoid alcohol 48h after.

  • Severely reduced absorption. Space 2-4 hours.
  • Ciprofloxacin + QT-prolonging drugs:
  • ️ Increased cardiac risk.
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ ORS: Dissolve sachet in cold water — sip after each loose stool
  • ️ Metronidazole: No alcohol during and 48h after
  • ️ Ciprofloxacin: Take 2h before or after antacids/dairy
  • ️ If bloody diarrhea or high fever → seek urgent care
  • Take Buscopan with food for cramps
  • ️ BRAT diet can help recovery
  • Do NOT use Loperamide if fever or blood in stool

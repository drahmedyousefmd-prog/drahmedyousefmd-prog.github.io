Hypertension

BP ≥ 140/90 mmHg on repeated measurements — most common CV risk factor

الرئيسية/Cardiovascular/Hypertension
التشخيص
  • - BP ≥ 140/90 on 2+ occasions, 2+ visits
  • - Check both arms, seated, after 5 min rest
  • - Target: < 140/90 (< 130/80 if diabetic/CKD)
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: FBS/HbA1c, lipid profile, creatinine/eGFR, electrolytes, urinalysis (proteinuria)
  • - ECG: 12-lead ECG — LVH, strain pattern, arrhythmia
  • - Imaging: Echocardiogram if HF suspected or uncontrolled HTN
  • - Monitor: Home BP monitoring, target < 140/90; renal function and K+ after starting ACEi/ARB
العلاج
  • - ACEi / ARB: Ramipril / Losartan — first choice
  • - CCB: Amlodipine — excellent for elderly
  • - Thiazide: HCTZ — if resistant HTN
  • - Beta-blocker: Bisoprolol — if tachycardia / HF
⚠ تحذير

Never combine ACEi + ARB — dual RAAS blockade increases risk of hyperkalemia and renal failure without benefit.

تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Take at the same time every day
  • ️ Ramipril: Start with 2.5mg, titrate gradually
  • ️ Do NOT use potassium supplements unless prescribed
  • ️ Report persistent dry cough (ACE inhibitor side effect — switch to ARB)
  • All can be taken with or without food
  • ️ Monitor blood pressure regularly at home
  • ️ Report dizziness when standing up
  • Do NOT stop suddenly — BP may rebound dangerously
  • Avoid excess salt — undermines medication effect

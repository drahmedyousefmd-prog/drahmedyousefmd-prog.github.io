Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia

Elderly male — nocturia, weak stream, urgency, retention

تحذير
Alpha-blockers can cause first-dose hypotension — take at bedtime. Avoid in patients on PDE5 inhibitors.
  • - IPSS score ≥ 8
  • - Urinary frequency, nocturia, weak stream, hesitancy
  • - Digital rectal exam: smooth, enlarged, non-tender prostate
  • - Lab: PSA level (with age reference), renal function; urinalysis to exclude UTI
  • - Imaging: Renal USS + post-void residual volume (PVR) — assess obstruction
  • - Special test: Uroflowmetry, IPSS symptom score
  • - Monitor: IPSS score, PVR, PSA trend, response to medical therapy
  • - Alpha-blocker: Tamsulosin 0.4mg — fast onset, first-line
  • - 5-ARI: Finasteride — shrinks prostate, takes 3–6 months
  • - Combo: Dutasteride + Tamsulosin — for large prostates

التفاعلات الدوائية
↑ Risk of hypotension. Separate by 4 hours.
Additive BP lowering. First-dose hypotension risk.
Finasteride-contaminated blood harmful to pregnant women. Don't donate for 1 month after stopping.
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Tamsulosin: Take AFTER evening meal — reduces dizziness
  • ️ First-dose hypotension: May feel dizzy — sit down
  • ️ Finasteride: Takes 3-6 months for full effect
  • ️ Finasteride: Pregnant women must NOT handle crushed tablets
  • Both can be taken long-term
  • ️ Report difficulty urinating, blood in urine, or urinary retention
  • Do NOT use Sildenafil/Tadalafil without telling BPH doctor

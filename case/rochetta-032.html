Hirsutism & Alopecia

Excess hair growth in females — hormonal vs idiopathic

  • - Ferriman-Gallwey score ≥ 8 = hirsutism
  • - Investigate: testosterone, DHEAS, LH/FSH ratio (PCOS)
  • - Sudden onset → ovarian/adrenal tumour → urgent work-up
  • - Lab: Total testosterone, free testosterone, DHEAS, LH/FSH ratio, 17-OHP
  • - Special test: Pelvic USS (PCOS, ovarian tumour); adrenal CT if DHEAS elevated
  • - Monitor: Ferriman-Gallwey score, menstrual cycle regularity, side effects of anti-androgens
  • - ECG: Baseline if on spironolactone (hyperkalaemia risk with ACEi)
  • - Cyproterone (Diane-35): 1st-line for hirsutism in females
  • - Spironolactone: 50–100mg — anti-androgen
  • - Minoxidil 5%: Topical — for androgenic alopecia

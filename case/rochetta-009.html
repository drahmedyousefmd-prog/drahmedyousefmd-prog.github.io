Mild-Moderate Pain

Headache, dental pain, sprains, post-op pain — paracetamol ± NSAIDs

تحذير
GI protection with NSAIDs: Add PPI if age > 65 or history of ulcer. Shortest duration possible. Avoid if GFR < 30 or active GI bleed. COX-2 inhibitors: lower GI risk, higher CV risk.
  • - WHO Pain Ladder: Mild → Paracetamol/NSAIDs
  • - Paracetamol: max 4g/day (2g if liver disease)
  • - NSAIDs: use lowest dose for shortest time
  • - Add topical NSAIDs before oral — less systemic effects
  • - Lab: CBC, ESR/CRP — assess inflammation if systemic cause suspected
  • - Imaging: X-ray or USS if trauma or joint involvement suspected
  • - Special test: Visual Analogue Scale (VAS) for pain severity; identify pain source
  • - Monitor: Renal function if recurrent NSAID use; stool occult blood if prolonged
  • - Ibuprofen: 400mg TID — first choice, OTC
  • - Diclofenac: 50mg TID — stronger, more GI risk
  • - Naproxen: 250-500mg BID — long-acting
  • - Celecoxib: 100-200mg BID — COX-2 selective, GI safe

Major Depressive Disorder

Persistent low mood, anhedonia, sleep/appetite changes — most common psychiatric disorder

الرئيسية/Psychiatry/Major Depressive Disorder
التشخيص
  • - 5+ symptoms for ≥ 2 weeks
  • - Low mood OR anhedonia (must have one)
  • - Sleep changes, appetite changes, fatigue
  • - Difficulty concentrating, psychomotor retardation
  • - Suicidal ideation — ALWAYS ask about this
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: CBC, FBS, HbA1c, LFTs, TSH, B12/folate — rule out organic causes of depression
  • - Special test: PHQ-9 severity score, Columbia suicide risk assessment
  • - Monitor: Weight, appetite, sleep pattern, suicidal ideation at each visit
  • - ECG: Baseline ECG if starting TCAs (amitriptyline) or with cardiac risk
العلاج
  • - 1st line: SSRI (Sertraline / Escitalopram)
  • - 2nd line: SNRI (Duloxetine / Venlafaxine)
  • - 3rd line: Mirtazapine (if insomnia + weight loss)
  • - If anxiety dominant: Escitalopram / Sertraline
  • - If insomnia dominant: Mirtazapine / Amitriptyline
  • - Minimum 4-6 weeks trial — full effect at 8-12 weeks
⚠ تحذير

Black box warning: SSRIs may increase suicidal ideation in patients < 25 years, especially in first 2–4 weeks. Monitor closely. NEVER stop SSRI abruptly — taper over 2–4 weeks.

تحذيرات إضافية
  • SSRIs (Sertraline/Escitalopram) + MAOIs:
  • Serotonin Syndrome — potentially fatal. NEVER combine. Washout period 2-4 weeks.
  • SSRIs + Tramadol / Triptans:
  • ️ Increased risk of Serotonin Syndrome. Avoid or use with extreme caution.
  • SSRIs + NSAIDs / Aspirin / Anticoagulants:
  • ️ Increased GI bleeding risk. Add PPI if combining.
  • Duloxetine + CYP1A2 inhibitors (Ciprofloxacin, Fluvoxamine):
  • ️ Increased Duloxetine levels. Monitor for side effects.
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Take at the same time daily — consistency matters
  • ️ Do NOT stop suddenly — taper over 2-4 weeks to avoid withdrawal
  • ️ Full therapeutic effect takes 4-8 weeks — don't judge too early
  • ️ Metallic taste is common in first 2 weeks — usually subsides
  • Sertraline: Take with or without food
  • Escitalopram: Take with or without food
  • Avoid alcohol — increases sedation and liver burden
  • Avoid MAOIs (old antidepressants) — fatal interaction
  • ️ If you feel worse or have suicidal thoughts in first 4 weeks, contact your doctor immediately

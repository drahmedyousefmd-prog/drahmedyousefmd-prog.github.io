H. pylori Eradication

Dyspepsia, epigastric pain — triple/quadruple therapy

الرئيسية/GIT/H. pylori Eradication
التشخيص
  • - Functional dyspepsia unresponsive to PPI
  • - Gastric / duodenal ulcer
  • - MALT lymphoma
  • - Test: urea breath test, stool Ag, or endoscopy biopsy
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Culture: Urea breath test (UBT) or stool H. pylori antigen — confirm eradication post-treatment
  • - Special test: Endoscopy with biopsy if alarm features or refractory dyspepsia
  • - Lab: CBC (iron deficiency from chronic gastritis), FBS if on long-term PPI
  • - Monitor: Confirm eradication 4+ weeks after completing therapy
العلاج
  • - First-line: Bismuth Quadruple Therapy (14 days)
  • - Second-line: Levofloxacin Triple Therapy (14 days)
  • - Third-line: Standard Triple Therapy (14 days)
  • - Clarithromycin resistance now exceeds 20-30% in many regions
⚠ تحذير

PPI is essential in all regimens — raises gastric pH and improves antibiotic efficacy. Always confirm eradication 4+ weeks after treatment completion.

⚠ التفاعلات الدوائية

❌ ↑↑ Rhabdomyolysis risk. Stop statin during treatment.

❌ Reduced antiplatelet effect. Use Pantoprazole instead.

❌ Disulfiram-like reaction. Avoid alcohol 48h after last dose.

تحذيرات إضافية
  • Clarithromycin + Statins (Simvastatin):
  • ↑↑ Rhabdomyolysis risk. Stop statin during treatment.
  • PPI (Omeprazole) + Clopidogrel:
  • Reduced antiplatelet effect. Use Pantoprazole instead.
  • Metronidazole + Alcohol:
  • Disulfiram-like reaction. Avoid alcohol 48h after last dose.
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Complete ALL 14 days — eradication depends on full course
  • ️ PPI: Take 30-60 min BEFORE meals for maximum effect
  • ️ Amoxicillin + Clarithromycin: Take WITH food
  • ️ Metronidazole: Do NOT drink alcohol — severe nausea/vomiting
  • ️ Avoid alcohol for 48h after last Metronidazole dose
  • ️ Repeat urea breath test 4 weeks after finishing
  • Do NOT skip doses — promotes resistance

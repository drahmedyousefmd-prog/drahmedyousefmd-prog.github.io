Rosacea

Central facial erythema, telangiectasia, papulopustules

الرئيسية/Dermatology/Rosacea
التشخيص
  • - Central facial erythema, telangiectasia, papulopustules — no comedones (vs acne)
  • - Triggers: sun, heat, spicy food, alcohol
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Culture: Skin swab if pustules present (exclude bacterial folliculitis)
  • - Special test: Demodex mite assessment (Dermoscopy / skin scraping)
  • - Monitor: Erythema score, papulopustule count, response to doxycycline course
  • - Screen: Ocular involvement (blepharitis), hormonal profile if female (PCOS association)
العلاج
  • - Trigger avoidance + daily sunscreen — cornerstone
  • - Topical for mild-moderate; oral for moderate-severe papulopustular
⚠ تحذير

Avoid topical steroids — they worsen / cause rebound rosacea.

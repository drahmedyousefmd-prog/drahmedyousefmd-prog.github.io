Erectile Dysfunction

Inability to achieve/maintain erection — assess cardiovascular risk

الرئيسية/Urinary/Erectile Dysfunction
التشخيص
  • - Check cardiovascular risk factors first
  • - Review medications (beta-blockers, SSRIs, antiandrogens)
  • - Hormonal: check testosterone, prolactin, TSH
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: Total testosterone (morning), prolactin, TSH, FSH/LH, fasting lipids, FBS, HbA1c
  • - ECG: Cardio risk assessment — coronary disease screen (stress test if risk factors)
  • - Special test: Glucose tolerance; penile duplex/ultrasound if vascular or anatomical cause
  • - Monitor: BP, lipid profile, cardiovascular risk, response to PDE5 inhibitors
العلاج
  • - Sildenafil: 50–100mg, 30–60 min before, with food
  • - Tadalafil: 10–20mg PRN or 5mg daily
  • - Start with lowest effective dose
⚠ تحذير

Absolute contraindication: PDE5 inhibitors + nitrates (GTN) = severe hypotension — potentially fatal.

تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️⚠️⚠️ NEVER take with ANY form of Nitrates (GTN, Isosorbide) — FATAL
  • ️ Tell doctor about ALL medications before starting PDE5 inhibitor
  • ️ Sildenafil: Take 30-60 min before, empty stomach for faster effect
  • ️ Tadalafil: Can be taken with food. Effect lasts up to 36h
  • ️ Start with lowest dose
  • ️ Sexual stimulation is required — not automatic
  • ️ If erection >4h (priapism) → go to ER immediately

  • Avoid grapefruit juice
  • ️ Report sudden vision or hearing loss → stop and seek care

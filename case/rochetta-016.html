Osteoarthritis

Mechanical joint pain, stiffness, reduced function — most common arthritis

  • - Weight-bearing joint pain worse with activity
  • - Morning stiffness < 30 min (vs RA > 1h)
  • - Crepitus, bony swelling, reduced ROM
  • - X-ray: joint space narrowing, osteophytes
  • - Imaging: Weight-bearing X-ray of affected joint (Kellgren–Lawrence grading)
  • - Lab: ESR/CRP, RF, anti-CCP — exclude inflammatory arthritis
  • - Special test: Functional assessment — ROM measurement, gait analysis, ADL scoring
  • - Monitor: Pain score (VAS), functional status, BMI (weight management)
  • - Non-pharm: Weight loss, exercise, physiotherapy
  • - Topical: NSAID gel (Diclofenac gel) — 1st line
  • - Oral: Paracetamol ± oral NSAID + PPI
  • - Intra-articular: Steroid injection — moderate relief

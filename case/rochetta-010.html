Neuropathic Pain

Burning, shooting, tingling — diabetic neuropathy, post-herpetic, sciatica

الرئيسية/Pain & Analgesics/Neuropathic Pain
التشخيص
  • - Burning, shooting, tingling pain in dermatomal distribution
  • - Allodynia (pain from normally non-painful stimulus)
  • - Common: diabetic neuropathy, post-herpetic neuralgia
  • - Standard analgesics (NSAIDs/paracetamol) often ineffective
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: FBS/HbA1c, B12, TSH — exclude diabetic neuropathy, B12 deficiency, hypothyroidism
  • - Special test: Nerve conduction study (NCS) if diagnosis uncertain; monofilament test (diabetic)
  • - Imaging: MRI spine if radiculopathy suspected (disc herniation)
  • - Monitor: Pain score (VAS), medication side effects, sedation level, weight
العلاج
  • - 1st line: Pregabalin 75mg BID → 150mg BID
  • - OR Gabapentin 300mg TID → 300mg TID
  • - OR Amitriptyline 10-25mg HS → 75mg HS
  • - OR Duloxetine 30mg OD → 60mg OD
  • - Titrate slowly over 2-4 weeks — aim for ≥50% pain relief
⚠ تحذير

Pregabalin/Gabapentin: Start low, titrate slow. Sedation and dizziness common. Do NOT stop abruptly — taper over 1 week. Slightly addictive — monitor.

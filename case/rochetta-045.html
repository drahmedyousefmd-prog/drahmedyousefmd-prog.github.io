Diabetes Mellitus

Fasting glucose ≥ 126 or HbA1c ≥ 6.5% — metabolic management

الرئيسية/Cardiovascular/Diabetes Mellitus
التشخيص
  • - Fasting glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL (2 readings)
  • - HbA1c ≥ 6.5%
  • - Random glucose ≥ 200 + symptoms
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: FBS, HbA1c (baseline + every 3 months), renal function, urinalysis (microalbuminuria)
  • - Lab: Lipid profile, LFTs (fatty liver), electrolytes — annual screening
  • - Special test: OGTT if diagnosis uncertain; retinal screening (annual); foot exam (monofilament)
  • - Monitor: Home glucose diary, HbA1c target < 7%, weight, BP (< 130/80)
العلاج
  • - Step 1: Metformin 500→1000mg BID + Lifestyle
  • - Step 2: + SGLT2i or GLP-1 RA (if HF/CKD/obesity)
  • - Step 3: + Sulfonylurea or DPP-4i or TZD
  • - Step 4: Add Insulin — start basal (Glargine)
  • - Target HbA1c < 7%

⚠ التفاعلات الدوائية

⚠️ Risk of lactic acidosis. STOP Metformin before contrast and restart 48h after.

⚠️ ↑ Hypoglycemia risk. Monitor glucose.

⚠️ Masks hypoglycemia symptoms. Glucose monitoring essential.

⚠️ ↑ Lactic acidosis risk. Avoid excessive alcohol.

تحذيرات إضافية
  • Metformin + Iodinated contrast media:
  • ️ Risk of lactic acidosis. STOP Metformin before contrast and restart 48h after.
  • Sulfonylureas (Gliclazide) + Fluconazole:
  • ️↑ Hypoglycemia risk. Monitor glucose.
  • Insulin/Sulfonylureas + Beta-blockers (non-selective):
  • ️ Masks hypoglycemia symptoms. Glucose monitoring essential.
  • Metformin + Alcohol:
  • ️↑ Lactic acidosis risk. Avoid excessive alcohol.
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Metformin: Take WITH food — reduces GI side effects
  • ️ Start with 500mg, increase gradually
  • ️ Gliclazide: Take BEFORE meals
  • ️ Regular blood glucose monitoring — keep a log
  • ️ HbA1c target: <7% — check every 3 months
  • ️ STOP Metformin 48h before/after contrast dye
  • ️ If vomiting/diarrhea — temporarily stop Metformin
  • Dapagliflozin: Take in the morning
  • ️ If on SGLT2i: report excessive thirst, frequent urination
  • ️ Always carry fast-acting sugar for hypoglycemia
  • Avoid excessive alcohol — lactic acidosis risk

Vitiligo

Depigmented chalk-white macules — autoimmune destruction of melanocytes

الرئيسية/Dermatology/Vitiligo
التشخيص
  • - Well-demarcated depigmented (chalk-white) macules/patches
  • - Symmetric distribution common — hands, face, genitals
  • - Wood's lamp: enhances contrast, confirms depigmentation
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: TSH, TPO antibodies (associated autoimmune thyroiditis), B12
  • - Special test: Wood's lamp examination; skin biopsy if atypical distribution
  • - Monitor: Repigmentation progress (months), response to steroid/calcineurin inhibitor
  • - Screen: Other autoimmune disease (vitiligo association)
العلاج
  • - Localized: topical steroid or calcineurin inhibitor
  • - Widespread: phototherapy (NB-UVB) — referral
  • - Sun protection essential — depigmented skin burns easily
⚠ تحذير

Screen for associated autoimmune disease (thyroid). Set realistic expectations — repigmentation is slow (months).

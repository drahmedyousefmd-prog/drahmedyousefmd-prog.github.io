Seborrheic Dermatitis

Greasy yellow scaly patches — scalp, face, chest

الرئيسية/Dermatology/Seborrheic Dermatitis
التشخيص
  • - Greasy yellow scaly patches — scalp, nasolabial folds, eyebrows, chest
  • - Chronic relapsing course — worse in winter and stress
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Special test: KOH preparation / fungal microscopy of scales (Malassezia)
  • - Culture: Fungal culture if atypical or unresponsive
  • - Lab: HIV screen if severe, refractory adult case
  • - Monitor: Response to antifungal therapy, adherence, recurrence frequency
العلاج
  • - Antifungal shampoo/cream (Malassezia role)
  • - Mild topical steroid for inflamed areas — short-term
⚠ تحذير

Severe/refractory cases in adults — consider HIV screening.

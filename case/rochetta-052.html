Overactive Bladder

Urgency, frequency, nocturia, urge incontinence

الرئيسية/Urinary/Overactive Bladder
التشخيص
  • - Urgency — sudden compelling desire to void
  • - Frequency — > 8 times per 24 hours
  • - Nocturia — waking ≥ 2 times to void
  • - Diagnosis of exclusion — rule out UTI, diabetes, BPH
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: Urinalysis + MSU culture; FBS/HbA1c, renal function, electrolytes
  • - Special test: Bladder diary (frequency/volume), post-void residual, uroflowmetry
  • - Monitor: Symptom score (urgency, frequency, nocturia), response to anticholinergic
  • - Screen: BPH (PSA/DRE in men), diabetes, UTI as underlying causes
العلاج
  • - Step 1: Bladder training + fluid management
  • - Step 2: Anticholinergic (Oxybutynin / Tolterodine)
  • - Step 3: Switch to Solifenacin (better tolerated)
  • - Step 4: Beta-3 agonist (Mirabegron) or Botulinum toxin
⚠ تحذير

Oxybutynin causes significant dry mouth, constipation, and cognitive side effects in elderly — prefer Tolterodine or Solifenacin.

  • ️ Urinate on schedule (timed voiding) for best results

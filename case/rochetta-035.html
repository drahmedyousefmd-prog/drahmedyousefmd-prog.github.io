Contact Dermatitis

Delayed (allergic) or immediate (irritant) skin reaction at contact site

الرئيسية/Dermatology/Contact Dermatitis
التشخيص
  • - Well-demarcated erythema/eczema at site of contact with allergen/irritant
  • - Allergic: delayed 24-48h, itchy; Irritant: immediate, burning
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Special test: Patch testing (allergic contact) to identify causative allergen
  • - Culture: Skin swab if secondary infection suspected (weeping, crusted)
  • - Lab: CBC, IgE if atopic diathesis suspected
  • - Monitor: Response to steroid + emollient, avoid recurrence by removing trigger
العلاج
  • - Identify and remove offending agent — key to management
  • - Topical steroid for acute flare + emollients for barrier repair
⚠ تحذير

Avoid potent steroids on face/flexures beyond short course. Patch testing if recurrent/unclear trigger.

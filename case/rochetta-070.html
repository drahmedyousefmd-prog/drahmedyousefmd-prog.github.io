Inflammatory Bowel Disease

Confirmed UC / Crohn's — supportive role of general practice

الرئيسية/GIT/Inflammatory Bowel Disease
التشخيص
  • - Mesalazine (5-ASA) — first line for mild-moderate UC
  • - Sulfasalazine — cheaper alternative, more side effects
  • - Needs endoscopic / pathology confirmation before treatment
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Biopsy: Endoscopy with colonoscopy and pathology — confirm UC or Crohn's diagnosis
  • - Lab: FBC (anaemia), ESR/CRP, faecal calprotectin, renal function, LFTs, electrolytes
  • - Culture: Stool MC&S to exclude infective colitis before starting therapy
  • - Monitor: Stool frequency, weight, abdominal symptoms, response to mesalazine
العلاج
  • - Recognize the pattern and refer to GI specialist
  • - Supportive: nutrition, symptom monitoring
  • - Not the setting to start immunosuppressive therapy
⚠ تحذير

Sulfasalazine reduces folic acid absorption — supplement separately.

الأمراض الجلدية

Acne Vulgaris

Comedones, papules, pustules — especially in adolescents and young adults

تحذير
Isotretinoin: Teratogenic — contraception mandatory. Monitor LFTs and lipids monthly. Avoid vitamin A supplements. Dry skin/lips very common — emollients essential.
  • - Grade I: Comedones (open/closed) — topical Rx
  • - Grade II: Papules & pustules — topical ± oral ABx
  • - Grade III: Nodules + papules — oral Isotretinoin
  • - Grade IV: Cystic/scarring — urgent referral
  • - Lab: FBS/HbA1c, LFTs, lipid profile — baseline before Isotretinoin
  • - Special test: Acne lesion count, severity grading (IGA scale)
  • - Monitor: Monthly LFTs, lipids, weight, mood assessment on Isotretinoin
  • - ECG: Not routinely; consider if Isotretinoin + cardiac history
  • - Topical: Adapalene/Tretinoin, Benzoyl Peroxide, Clindamycin
  • - Systemic: Doxycycline 100mg OD × 6–12 weeks (Grade II–III)
  • - Isotretinoin: 0.5–1mg/kg/day × 16–24 weeks (Grade III–IV)
  • - OCP (females): Combined pill for hormonal acne

التفاعلات الدوائية
Reduced absorption. Space 2-4 hours.
May reduce OCP effectiveness. Use additional contraception.
↑↑ Hypervitaminosis A. Stop all Vitamin A supplements.
Increased intracranial pressure (pseudotumor cerebri). NEVER combine.
تعليمات الاستخدام
  • ️ Isotretinoin: CONTRACEPTION MANDATORY — teratogenic
  • ️ Start contraception 1 month before, during, and 1 month after
  • ️ Monthly pregnancy tests for females
  • ️ Monthly LFT and lipid monitoring on Isotretinoin
  • ️ Doxycycline: Take with food, avoid sun exposure
  • Adapalene: Apply thin layer at NIGHT
  • Benzoyl Peroxide: Apply morning and evening — can bleach fabrics
  • ️ Initial worsening with retinoids is normal (2-4 weeks)
  • Do NOT take Vitamin A supplements on Isotretinoin
  • ️ Moisturize regularly — dry skin/lips very common

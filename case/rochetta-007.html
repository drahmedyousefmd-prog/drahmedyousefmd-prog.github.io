Alcohol Dependence & Withdrawal

Compulsive use despite harm — withdrawal can be life-threatening

الرئيسية/Psychiatry/Alcohol Dependence & Withdrawal
التشخيص
  • - 6–12h: tremor, anxiety, sweating, nausea
  • - 12–24h: visual/auditory hallucinations
  • - 24–48h: seizures — may be severe
  • - 48–96h: Delirium tremens (mortality 5% if untreated)
الفحوصات والتشخيص المخبري
  • - Lab: CBC, LFTs (AST:ALT >2), GGT, MCV (macrocytosis), glucose, electrolytes, magnesium
  • - Special test: CDT (carbohydrate-deficient transferrin), AUDIT-C screening
  • - Monitor: CIWA-Ar score every 1–4h during withdrawal, vitals, fluid balance
  • - Culture: Blood cultures if fever during withdrawal to exclude sepsis
العلاج
  • - Benzodiazepine: Diazepam — symptom-triggered dosing
  • - Thiamine: IV before glucose — prevent Wernicke's
  • - Maintenance: Acamprosate / Disulfiram / Naltrexone

⚠ التفاعلات الدوائية

❌ Disulfiram-like reaction: nausea, vomiting, flushing, tachycardia. Avoid alcohol 48h after last dose.

❌ Severe reaction: hypotension, respiratory depression, death. Absolutely no alcohol.

❌ Precipitates opioid withdrawal. Ensure opioid-free for 7-10 days.
